About Mr. Klin

Car care done right, right here in Taguig.

Mr. Klin Trading and Services Corp. is your one-stop auto shop in the South. Detailing, ceramic coating, mechanical work, body repair, paint and accessories all happen under one roof, so your car gets everything it needs in one visit.

Who we are

One shop for everything your car needs.

Most car owners juggle a car wash, a detailer, a mechanic and a body shop, each in a different part of town. Mr. Klin puts all of that in one place. Bring your car to our shop on M.L Quezon St. in Bagumbayan, Taguig, and the same team can clean it, protect it, fix it and upgrade it.

We work on every kind of vehicle, from city sedans and hatchbacks to SUVs, pickups, vans and sports cars. Our prices follow your vehicle's size, so you only pay for the car you bring. If you can't come to us, our mobile team brings the detailing to your home or office.

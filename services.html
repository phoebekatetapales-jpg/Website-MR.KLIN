Good to know

Questions we often get.

How do you price your services?

Detailing is priced by vehicle size, so a hatchback costs less than a van. Coating, mechanical, body and accessory work depends on your car's condition, so we give you a quote after a quick inspection.

Can you come to my home or office?

Yes. Our mobile team can do detailing at your home or office. Choose "Home / office service" when you book online and tell us your address.

How do I book?

Book online with our estimate and calendar, or call 0917-880-5755. We'll text or call you to confirm your slot and price.

Can I reach you on Sundays?

Yes. Call our Sunday line at 0933-866-7408.

Will the price change after I book?

The online price is an estimate. If your car needs extra work, like deep shampooing for heavy stains, we'll tell you and agree on it with you before doing it.

Not sure what your car needs?

Tell us about your car and we'll recommend the right service and give you an estimate.
